# Third-party artwork

## Bathurst / Mount Panorama outline

`assets/tracks/bathurst.svg` and the matching inline track SVG in `gui/controller.html` are adapted from **Mount Panorama street racing circuit in Australia.svg**, by **Will Pittenger** (17 October 2008; direction correction 19 October 2008).

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mount_Panorama_street_racing_circuit_in_Australia.svg
- Licence: **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
- Changes: extracted the current circuit centreline (`path3182`), removed labels, other roads and decoration, uniformly scaled and translated its coordinates into the controller viewBox, and changed stroke colour and width.
- The adapted outline remains licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0. This artwork licence applies to the outline, not the separately licensed application code. No endorsement is implied.

The outline is schematic; lap-distance markers are interpolated along the SVG. This does not establish precise geographic positions or live-session calibration.
